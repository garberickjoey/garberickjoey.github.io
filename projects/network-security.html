Security principles

Segmentation reduces unnecessary lateral movement. By grouping VLANs and interfaces into zones, you are gain granular control over traffic between the zones. Some basic examples of zones would be Internal, External, DMZ, VPN, and Guest. Internal can home a group of trusted internal networks, whereas External would contain non-trusted WAN/Internet networks. The DMZ (demillitarized zone) is a location to place Internet facing servers, such as a mail or web server. Guest would contain the untrusted internal networks, and VPN would contain remote VPN connections. Zone-Based Firewall policy should be explicit, documented, reviewed periodically, and based on business requirements rather than broad “allow all” rules.
