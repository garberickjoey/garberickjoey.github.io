Network Segmentation & Firewall Policy

A security-oriented network design using segmentation and least-privilege communication rules.

Policy examples

Source Zone Destination Zone Policy
Users Servers Allow only required services

Security principles

Segmentation reduces unnecessary lateral movement. Firewall policy should be explicit, documented, reviewed periodically, and based on business requirements rather than broad “allow all” rules.
